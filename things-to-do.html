Outdoor adventures and attractions

The lake is the main event. Get on the water, snag a fish, or walk the trail of the Roanoke River..

On the water

Boating

Pontoons, skiing, and sunset cruises

Bring your own or rent from one of the many marinas around the lake.

Fishing

Bass, crappie, and catfish

Pea Hill Creek, Summerville Branch, or the John H. Kerr Dam are among the best spots to catch fish.

Paddling

Kayaks and canoes

Several calm coves and rental locations are available around the lake.

On land

Hiking and walking

Trails near the lake

The Roanoke Canal Trail offers scenic walks through the surrounding landscape.

Lake Gaston Dam

A look at how the lake was made

Visit the dam for stunning views and learn about the history of Lake Gaston.

Lake towns

Beyond Littleton

Explore the charming small towns around Lake Gaston for unique experiences.
